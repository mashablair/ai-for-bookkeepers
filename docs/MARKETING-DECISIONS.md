# Framework review and implementation choices

Reviewed the requested frameworks in https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills (retrieved October 28, 2026): product-marketing, customer-research, offers, copywriting, copy-editing, cro, free-tools, lead-magnets, seo-audit, ai-seo, schema, analytics and conversion-tracking.

Applied: preserve the supplied v1.1 marketing context; explain audience and offer near the hero; keep a clear assessment-first CTA; publish transparent starting pricing; give results before optional capture; minimize lead fields; make successful persistence the conversion event; provide crawlable content, canonical metadata and conservative service schema.

Not applied: unconfirmed guarantees, bonuses, urgency, competitor comparisons, testimonials, invented customer findings, paid advertising infrastructure or extra funnel pages. These are unnecessary or contradict the authoritative context. Five-to-seven minutes remains a design target, not a measured claim. Customer interview and timing validation are still needed.

Photography: reused the three photographs referenced by https://mariablairai.com/ as the owner's supplied sister-brand reference. Fonts are bundled locally from Fontsource (Cormorant Garamond and Jost), with their package licenses retained in dependencies.

Implementation references: https://opennext.js.org/cloudflare/get-started ; https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/server-side/creating-a-client ; OpenAI Responses API and installed SDK types. No structured-data or AI-search inclusion guarantees are made.
