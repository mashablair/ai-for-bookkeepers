## 1. Product overview and positioning

**Established:** AI for Bookkeepers is a standalone AI training and consulting offering led by Maria Blair for independent bookkeepers and owners of small bookkeeping practices serving private clients. It covers beginners and people already using ChatGPT or Claude. Its intended outcomes are saving time, improving workflows, and using AI confidently and responsibly.

**Founder credibility — established by owner confirmation:** Maria Blair has 11+ years of software engineering experience, works with AI, and has delivered personalized paid AI training. This provides relevant technical and teaching experience for the offer. No accounting credentials, bookkeeping qualifications, training client counts, or customer outcomes have been supplied.

**Category:** Personalized AI training and consulting for bookkeepers. A service offering; the assessment supports the service.

**Proposed one-liner:** Practical AI training for bookkeepers, built around what you already know and the work you need help with next.

**Proposed positioning:** For independent bookkeepers and small-practice owners who want practical help applying AI, AI for Bookkeepers offers personalized instruction informed by a pre-session skills assessment. Assessment-informed personalized teaching builds on what each bookkeeper already knows and focuses on what they need to learn next. Training connects those gaps to real tasks, with attention to confidentiality and accuracy checks.

**Proposed customer benefit:** Learn to apply AI to repetitive administrative work, such as drafting routine client communications, organizing documents, and preparing reusable SOP drafts, with the potential to reduce time spent on these tasks. The intended benefit is a more manageable workflow; actual results depend on the task, setup, and review required. No measured savings have been established.

These are intended benefits and a delivery approach. They are not guarantees of time savings, financial results, or error-free work.

## 2. Audience and jobs to be done

**Established primary audience:** Independent bookkeepers and small bookkeeping-practice owners serving private clients, ranging from AI beginners to current ChatGPT/Claude users.

**Established secondary offer audience:** Small teams or groups requesting workshops. Specific team size, geography, industry specialization, and budget thresholds are unknown.

**Buying-role hypothesis:** An independent practitioner may be both learner and purchaser. In a small practice, the owner may approve training for staff. Do not assume a complex buying committee.

**Proposed jobs to be done, requiring customer validation:**

- Identify useful AI skills to learn next without repeating familiar material.
- Apply AI to a recurring practice task with clear instructions and review steps, potentially reducing time spent on repetitive administrative work.
- Build confidence in deciding when AI is useful and how to check its work.

**Established training topic areas:** Client communications, document organization, SOPs, spreadsheet/data analysis, reporting, and advisory support, alongside prompting, projects/workspaces, instructions, sources, and relevant integrations. Topic inclusion depends on the learner's needs; these are not promises of finished automation or accounting advice.

## 5. Differentiation and alternatives

**Proposed lead differentiator:** Teach what each bookkeeper needs next, building on the skills they already have. The pre-session assessment informs a personalized agenda, and Maria reviews it before delivery. For someone already using projects or workspaces, this means exploring the effectiveness of instructions and sources rather than automatically reteaching setup. For a beginner, it means starting with appropriate fundamentals.

**Established design choices with proposed marketing value:**

- Assessment-informed personalized teaching: identify existing strengths, focus on relevant gaps, and build on prior knowledge so training time addresses the learner's next steps.
- Bookkeeping context: connect instruction to practice tasks rather than abstract tool features.
- Starting-point awareness: adapt to ChatGPT, Claude, both, or neither, and investigate the quality of existing setups.
- Human review: Maria reviews the draft agenda before delivery.
- Responsible use: address confidentiality, accuracy checks, and handling of client information as training topics.

These explain the intended approach. They do not establish uniqueness, superiority, or why customers have chosen the service.

**Alternative categories to research:** Other AI trainers, general AI courses/tutorials, self-directed experimentation, peer support, and continuing existing workflows. No named competitors or verified shortcomings have been established. Do not claim alternatives are ineffective or lack personalization without evidence.

**Switching hypotheses:** Repeated unsuccessful experiments may create interest; relevance and preparation may attract learners; familiar routines may delay action; concerns about time, cost, privacy, and accuracy may inhibit purchase.

## 6. Objections and fit

All objections below are anticipated hypotheses, not objections heard in sales.

| Possible objection | Proposed response grounded in the offer |
|---|---|
| I already use AI. | The assessment examines current capabilities so the agenda can focus on gaps and more effective use. |
| I do not know where to start. | The assessment accommodates people using neither ChatGPT nor Claude. |
| I am unsure whether paid training is worthwhile. | Review the free results first; requesting training is optional. Explain the hourly rate and agree scope before booking. |
| I cannot share client financial information. | The assessment should not collect actual client financial records. Confirm appropriate materials and handling before training; do not promise unverified security controls. |

**Proposed fit boundary:** People seeking personalized learning are the intended audience. Fully managed automation, outsourced bookkeeping, certification, or guaranteed financial outcomes are not documented offers.

## 7. Brand voice and customer language

**Proposed voice:** Approachable, practical, clear, respectful, and credible. This direction reflects the project brief; it has not been tested against performing copy or customer language.

| Attribute | Writing guidance | Draft example, not a customer quote |
|---|---|---|
| Approachable | Welcome different starting points without condescension. | Start with what you know. Find out what to learn next. |
| Practical | Name the task and next action. | Build your training agenda around the work you do each week. |
| Clear | Use familiar words and explain tool terminology. | See your strengths, gaps, and recommended next steps. |
| Respectful | Recognize existing expertise and keep training optional. | Already using AI? Focus on the skills you want to develop. |
| Credible | Describe the process; avoid inflated outcomes. | Maria reviews your draft training agenda before your session. |

**Tone by context:** Website copy should be welcoming and specific; assessment questions neutral and nonjudgmental; results constructive; consultation invitations direct and optional; error messages calm with a clear recovery step.

**Prefer:** practical AI skills, personalized training, bookkeeping practice, next steps, review, client confidentiality. **Avoid:** revolutionary, effortless, guaranteed savings, error-free, certified, or unsupported claims about being the best or only provider.

**Customer language:** No verified customer quotations are available. Do not present draft headlines, imagined objections, or paraphrases as testimonials. No proprietary terminology has been established.

## 8. Customer journey and conversion goals

The following is the planned journey, not a report of a live funnel:

1. **Discover:** Arrive through Anna's referrals or organic discovery; additional industry partnerships are a proposed future channel.
2. **Understand:** Learn who the service is for, relevant use cases, training formats, and the initial hourly rate.
3. **Assess:** Complete the free adaptive assessment, targeting 5–7 minutes, without payment or a booking requirement.
4. **Receive value:** See strengths, gaps, and tailored recommendations independently of a purchase.
5. **Request help optionally:** Submit a consultation or workshop request with appropriate contact details. Booking integration and consent details remain open.
6. **Prepare and deliver:** Maria reviews submitted responses and the draft agenda, confirms priorities and scope, and provides tailored training.

**Established website requirements:** A public marketing page, assessment and results experience, and a private consultant dashboard. Avoid actual client financial records in the assessment. Dashboard security and data practices must be implemented and verified before making public claims about them.

**Proposed primary CTA:** Take the free AI skills assessment.  
**Proposed secondary CTA:** Request personalized training.  
**Workshop CTA:** Request a workshop.

**Proposed measurement:** Track assessment starts, completions, successful consultation/workshop submissions, referral source, and eventual paying clients. Measure successful submissions separately from button clicks. Define qualified leads and attribution rules before reporting conversion rates. Baselines, targets, revenue, and acquisition costs are unknown.
